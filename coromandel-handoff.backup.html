04 / The Edit

Content vs. Code

It is important to distinguish between updating information and altering the digital structure.

Content Changes

Handled directly by the Coromandel team in Sanity Studio. This includes text, imagery, team members, projects, showreels, and publishing pages.

Development Changes

Require code updates and deployment by Lune. This includes new page layouts, interactive functionality, structural design alterations, and third-party integrations.

If you are unsure whether a change requires Sanity or code, contact Lune.

06 / Next Frame

The First 30 Days

A website is a living asset. Here is a sensible rhythm for the weeks immediately following launch.

Week 01 — Settle In

Circulate the new website internally and to close partners. Ensure everyone is using the correct URLs.

Week 02 — Build Signal

Update Google Business Profile activity and ensure social links point to the new domain.

Week 03 — Add Proof

Publish selected recent work and begin collecting new client reviews to support the launch.

Week 04 — Review

Review early analytics, monitor enquiry behaviour, and identify opportunities for the next iteration.

Analytics Guidance

Focus on signal, not noise. Watch these core metrics in Google Analytics:

  • 01 Visitors
    Are people finding the site?
  • 02 Top Pages
    What work is receiving attention?
  • 03 Traffic Sources
    Where are visitors coming from?
  • 04 Geography
    Which markets are showing interest?
  • 05 Enquiry Actions
    Are visitors moving toward contact forms or Calendly?
